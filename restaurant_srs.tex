% ── 1. INTRODUCTION ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Purpose}
This document specifies the functional requirements for a web-based Restaurant Management
Application. It serves as the reference for development, describing what the system must do
from the perspective of its two user roles: Admin and Employee (Staff).

\subsection{Scope}
The application will allow a restaurant owner (Admin) to manage staff accounts, recipes,
menu items, and inventory, while giving employees the ability to clock in/out and view
relevant information. The system is a web application built with a Java Spring Boot backend
and a relational database.

\subsection{Definitions}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\bfseries}p{2cm} X}
\toprule
\headerrow Term & Definition \\
\midrule
\rowcolor{lightgray} Admin       & The restaurant owner or manager. Has full access to the system. \\
Employee    & A staff member. Has limited, read/clock access. \\
\rowcolor{lightgray} Ingredient  & A raw material used in recipes (e.g.\ eggs, chicken). \\
Inventory   & Tracks the current stock quantity of each ingredient. \\
\rowcolor{lightgray} Recipe      & A named set of ingredients with quantities and a preparation procedure. \\
Item        & A menu item sold to customers. Linked to a Recipe and has a price. \\
\rowcolor{lightgray} TimeSheet   & A record of an employee clock-in and clock-out for a given day. \\
Threshold   & A minimum stock level for an ingredient. Triggers a low-stock alert when crossed. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

% ── 2. OVERALL DESCRIPTION ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Overall Description}

\subsection{System Overview}
The Restaurant Management Application is a role-based web system. Upon login, users are
directed to a dashboard appropriate to their role. The Admin dashboard provides full
management capabilities. The Employee dashboard provides limited operational access.

\subsection{User Roles}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\bfseries}p{2.5cm} X}
\toprule
\headerrow Role & Description \\
\midrule
\rowcolor{lightgray} Admin    & Full access: manage staff, recipes, inventory, items, and view reports. \\
Employee & Limited access: clock in/out, view assigned recipes, view inventory levels. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

% ── 5. APPLICATION LAYER DESIGN ─────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Application Layer Design}

\subsection{Data Transfer Objects (DTOs)}

DTOs decouple the API contract from the database entities. Request DTOs exclude auto-generated
fields (e.g.\ \texttt{id}, timestamps) and accept foreign keys as plain IDs rather than full
objects. Response DTOs include the generated \texttt{id} so the client can use it in subsequent
requests.

\subsubsection*{Request DTOs}

\begin{longtable}{>{\bfseries\ttfamily}p{5.2cm} p{8cm}}
\toprule
\headerrow Class & Fields \\
\midrule
\endhead
\rowcolor{lightgray} LoginRequest & userName, password \\
CreateUserRequest & name, userName, password \\
\rowcolor{lightgray} CreateIngredientRequest & name, costPricePerUnit, unit (Unit enum) \\
UpdateIngredientRequest & name, costPricePerUnit, unit (Unit enum) \\
\rowcolor{lightgray} RecipeIngredientRequest & ingredientId (Long), quantity (int) \\
CreateRecipeRequest & name, procedure, ingredients (List\textless RecipeIngredientRequest\textgreater) \\
\rowcolor{lightgray} UpdateRecipeRequest & name, procedure, ingredients (List\textless RecipeIngredientRequest\textgreater) \\
CreateItemRequest & name, price (double), recipeId (Long) \\
\rowcolor{lightgray} UpdateItemRequest & name, price (double), recipeId (Long) \\
CreateInventoryRequest & ingredientId (Long), quantity (int), threshold (int) \\
\rowcolor{lightgray} UpdateInventoryRequest & quantity (int), threshold (int) \\
ClockInRequest & userId (Long) \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\textcolor{midgray}{\small Note: Clock-out is handled via a \texttt{PATCH} endpoint using the timesheet \texttt{id} with no request body; the server records the current time automatically.}

\vspace{1em}

\subsection{Repositories}

All repositories extend \texttt{JpaRepository\textless Entity, Long\textgreater}, providing built-in
\texttt{save()}, \texttt{findById()}, \texttt{findAll()}, and \texttt{deleteById()} methods.
Custom query methods are listed below.

\begin{longtable}{>{\bfseries\ttfamily}p{5.2cm} p{8cm}}
\toprule
\headerrow Repository & Custom Methods \\
\midrule
\endhead
\rowcolor{lightgray} UserRepository & \texttt{findByUserName(String)} --- used for login authentication \\
IngredientRepository & none --- base CRUD sufficient \\
\rowcolor{lightgray} RecipeRepository & none --- base CRUD sufficient \\
RecipeIngredientRepository & \texttt{findByRecipeId(Long)} --- fetch all ingredients for a recipe \\
\rowcolor{lightgray} ItemRepository & none --- base CRUD sufficient \\
InventoryRepository & \texttt{findByIngredientId(Long)} --- look up stock for a specific ingredient \\
\rowcolor{lightgray} TimeSheetRepository & \texttt{findByUserId(Long)} --- fetch all shift records for an employee \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
